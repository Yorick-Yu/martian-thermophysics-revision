SOURCE DATA 1
This archive supports the revised observational analysis, Tables 1-3 and Figures 1-10.

audit/raw_projection.json: both projected model branches, observed two-band arrays, masks, calibration configurations, 125 PSF/registration perturbations per site and archived state-file hashes.
audit/analysis.json: corrected nominal/scale-dependent statistics, paired comparisons and uncertainty terms.
audit/bootstrap_128.json and audit/extra: the archived 128-grid Kaiser check.
results: numeric diagnostics and arrays used to draw the revised figures. Historical g_b3 and calibrated_b3 names denote the terrain-shadow column branch, not a full interfacet-radiation calculation.

Physical radiance unit: W cm^-2 sr^-1 micrometre^-1. The figures display explicit powers-of-ten scales. Valid masks must be retained.

Public original data: NASA PDS, https://pds.nasa.gov; HiRISE DTMs DTEEC_021584_1440_022217_1440_A01 and DTEEC_013017_1325_013083_1325_U01. THEMIS products: I89192002SNU, I97665004SNU, I88618002SNU, I89277002SNU, IA5830002SNU, IA5986002SNU, IA6273002SNU.
Credits: THEMIS data NASA/JPL/Arizona State University; HiRISE data NASA/JPL/University of Arizona. No original mission products are relicensed by this archive.

This is not the archive of every thermal-generation checkpoint or all original forcing assets. Full raw-state regeneration remains a separate provenance and availability scope.
